{% trans "3) The free browser extension" %}

{% trans "The extension has a single purpose: routing your browser traffic through an encrypted VPN proxy. It works without an account, so no personal data is required to use it." %}

{% trans "Permissions and what they're for:" %}

  • proxy — {% trans "routes browser traffic through the VPN. This is the product." %}
  • declarativeNetRequest — {% trans "optional on-device ad & tracker blocking. Filtering happens inside your browser; block decisions are never transmitted to us." %}
  • storage — {% trans "saves your preferences (location, auto-connect, ad-block toggle) and a random client identifier, locally in your browser." %}
  • tabs — {% trans "powers per-tab routing (choosing which tabs use the VPN). Page contents are never read." %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}In line with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy: we only use extension data to provide the VPN's single purpose, we transmit connection data over encrypted channels, and we do not sell, rent or transfer user data to third parties for advertising, credit-worthiness or any unrelated purpose. The extension's free tier is supported by ads we serve ourselves from our own server; no advertising SDK runs inside the extension and these ads involve no behavioral profiling — we have no browsing data to profile you with.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3.2) VPNsterr for Android" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}The Android app routes your device's traffic through an encrypted VPN tunnel using Android's VpnService. The zero-logs rules in section 2 apply to it in full: we do not record which apps you use, which sites you visit, your DNS queries, or your traffic.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Before the app makes its first network request, it shows a disclosure screen explaining what it will do. Nothing below happens until you accept it." %}

{% trans "What the app handles:" %}

  • {% trans "Device identifier:" %} {% trans "a random identifier generated on your device the first time you open the app. It is not your advertising ID, not your hardware ID, and cannot be traced back to you. It exists so a subscription can be tied to a device and device limits enforced." %}
  • {% trans "Account e-mail:" %} {% trans "only if you choose to sign in. Free use requires no account." %}
  • {% trans "Subscription status:" %} {% trans "your plan, its expiry date, and — for purchases made through Google Play — the purchase token, which we send to Google to confirm the subscription is genuine." %}
  • {% trans "Device model and app version:" %} {% trans "so you can recognise your own devices in the dashboard and we can support you." %}

{% trans "Android permissions and why they exist:" %}

  • VpnService / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE — {% trans "creates and maintains the tunnel. Android always asks for your explicit permission first, and the foreground notification exists so you can always tell the VPN is on." %}
  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — {% trans "connect, and notice when the network changes." %}
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — {% trans "the ongoing connection notification Android requires for a foreground service." %}
  • AD_ID — {% trans "the advertising identifier, used only for ads on the free tier. See the next section." %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}The app asks for no location permission, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no file access, and does not read the list of apps installed on your device.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3.3) Advertising in the Android app" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}The free tier of the Android app is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. This is the one place where a third party receives data about you, so we are specific about it.{% endblocktrans %}

  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}AdMob uses your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides), along with technical data such as device type, operating system, coarse region and language, to select and measure ads. It may serve personalised ads on this basis.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}Google acts as an independent controller for this data. How Google uses it is described in Google's own policy.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}We never send your VPN activity to any ad network — we do not have it. Ads are never targeted using anything you did through the VPN.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}Where your consent is legally required for personalised ads, the app asks on first launch and you can change your answer any time in its settings.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalisation across every app on the device.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %}Premium removes ads entirely. No ad is requested and no ad SDK data is sent while your subscription is active.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "The browser extension does not use AdMob or any other advertising SDK; its ads come from our own server and involve no profiling." %}
