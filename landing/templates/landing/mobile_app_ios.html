Why the iPhone app isn't out yet

A mobile VPN for iOS can't be a lighter port of the Android build. Apple routes all tunnelling through its Network Extension framework, which needs a specific entitlement granted per developer account, and the app has to be rebuilt against it rather than reusing the Android VPN service.

VPN apps also get a stricter App Store review than almost any other category. Apple requires the publisher to be a registered organisation rather than an individual, wants a clear statement of what the app collects, and rejects apps that resell someone else's network without saying so. That's a good thing for you and a slow thing for us.

So the honest position: the iOS client is being built, it has not been submitted, and we're not going to put a date on this page that we might miss. When it ships, this page becomes the download page.

How to judge a free VPN for iPhone

We're not going to pretend you'll wait for us. If you're hunting for the best free VPN for iPhone this week and installing something today, these are the five things actually worth checking — and most of them take under a minute on the App Store listing itself.

01

Read the privacy label, not the screenshots

Every App Store listing has an App Privacy section. If a "no-logs" VPN declares that it collects Browsing History or Identifiers linked to you, the marketing copy and the legal disclosure disagree — believe the disclosure.

02

Find out how it's paid for

Servers and bandwidth cost money every single month. If there are no ads, no subscription and no parent company with another product, you are the revenue. A free VPN that won't explain its funding has a reason.

03

Refuse configuration profiles

A legitimate iOS VPN asks for one permission, through the system dialog. If an app tells you to install a configuration profile from a website, stop — that mechanism is built for corporate device management and can hand over far more than a VPN needs.

04

Check what a data cap really means

Most free iPhone VPNs give you between 500 MB and 10 GB a month. On a phone that is a few evenings of video. A cap isn't dishonest — but an app advertising itself as free when it's a monthly trial is.

05

Look for a company, not a brand

Scroll to the developer name and look it up. The best mobile VPN for iPhone is rarely the one with the most stars — a VPN is the one app that sees all your traffic, so knowing which legal entity is on the other end, and which country it answers to, matters a great deal more.
